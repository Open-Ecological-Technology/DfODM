Open Ecological Technology · Design Framework

The Convivial Virtues

Aristotle's doctrine of the mean applied to appropriate technology across eight registers — from individual tool design to social structure to the six OSE specification clusters. Each virtue names the design optimum; the paired vices name failure modes on either side.
